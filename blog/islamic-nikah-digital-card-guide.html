Emerald Nikah & Islamic Digital Marriage Cards: Cultural Etiquette & Designs

Published by InviteStory Editorial · 5 min read · Updated August 2026

Islamic wedding celebrations (Nikah & Walima) carry rich spiritual tradition and grace. As couples transition from paper cards to digital web links, maintaining dignity, sacred calligraphic phrasing (such as Bismillah-ir-Rahman-ir-Rahim), and clear event details is paramount.

Design Features of Emerald Nikah Digital Cards

The Emerald Nikah template by InviteStory is specifically crafted for Muslim weddings with a refined aesthetic:

  • Rich Emerald Green & Antique Gold Palette: Symbolizing peace, divine grace, and celebration.
  • Geometric Arch Motif & Arabic Calligraphy: Intricate geometric patterns framing the bride and groom's names.
  • Separate Nikah & Walima Tabs: Clearly defining times, locations, and dress codes for both ceremonies.
  • Google Maps Location Links: Seamlessly guiding family and guests to the venue or Masjid.

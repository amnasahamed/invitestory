How to Track Wedding RSVPs & Guest Management Online Effortlessly

Published by InviteStory Editorial · 5 min read · Updated August 2026

Managing responses for hundreds of wedding guests across multiple pre-wedding ceremonies and receptions can quickly become chaotic. Digital RSVP tracking simplifies guest list management into a single live dashboard.

1. Collecting Detailed Event Attendance

Allow guests to select exactly which functions they will attend (e.g., Sangeet vs. Main Wedding vs. Reception). This ensures accurate catering plates per function.

2. Capturing Dietary Preferences & Plus-Ones

Custom RSVP forms can include fields for vegetarian/vegan requests, allergen alerts, and exact counts of attending family members.

3. Exporting Live Guest Reports for Caterers & Hotels

Instantly download structured response data to send directly to venue coordinators and hotel management for room allocation.

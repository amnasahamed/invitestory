Digital Save-the-Date Cards: When to Send Them & What Details to Include

Published by InviteStory Editorial · 5 min read · Updated August 2026

Master the timing, essential information, and aesthetic design tips for digital Save-the-Date notifications.

1. Why Send a Digital Save-the-Date Early?

Digital Save-the-Dates allow out-of-town guests to reserve travel dates and book flights 6 to 8 months in advance. Unlike physical cards, digital alerts can be updated in real-time as venue details finalize.

2. Essential Details to Include

Keep it concise: Couple names, wedding date, city/country location, and a teaser link to your full digital wedding website.

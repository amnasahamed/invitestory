The Ultimate Pre-Wedding Checklist: 10 Things to Prepare Before Creating Your Digital Invitation

Published by InviteStory Editorial · 5 min read · Updated August 2026

Creating a modern digital wedding invitation is one of the most exciting milestones in your wedding planning journey. Unlike printed paper cards, an interactive e-invite allows you to embed Google Maps, dynamic RSVP forms, multi-day schedules, and background audio. To make setting up your digital card seamless, here are the 10 essential pre-wedding items to get ready beforehand.

1. Finalized Date & Venue Map Links

Ensure your venue bookings are confirmed and grab exact Google Maps pin URLs. Providing precise map navigation inside your digital card guarantees out-of-town guests won't get lost on the big day.

2. Complete Multi-Event Schedule

List all wedding events chronologically—from Mehendi and Sangeet to the Ceremony and Reception. Include precise timings, dress code recommendations, and hall locations.

3. High-Resolution Couple Photos

Gather 3 to 8 high-quality portraits from your pre-wedding photoshoot. Digital templates showcase your love story through beautiful photo galleries or hero cover images.

4. Custom Music & Playlist Choice

Select your favorite instrumental song or romantic audio track to play automatically when guests open your digital invitation card.

5. Guest RSVP Deadline

Decide on your RSVP cut-off date (typically 3 to 4 weeks prior to the wedding). This gives your caterers and venue management an accurate headcount.

6. Accommodation & Travel Information

If hosting a destination wedding or providing hotel blocks for guests, note down check-in details, airport pickup contacts, and stay addresses.

7. Dress Code & Aesthetic Themes

Specify color palettes or attire guidelines (e.g., Pastel Ethnic, Black Tie, Traditional South Indian, Chic Evening Wear) so guests can plan their outfits early.

8. Family & Host Names

Coordinate with both families on how parents', grandparents', and hosts' names should be displayed on the digital invitation cover.

9. Gift Registry or 'No Gifts' Note

Decide whether you wish to include a registry link, QR code, or a polite note requesting warm blessings only.

10. Dedicated WhatsApp & Contact Number

Appoint a designated family member or wedding coordinator to receive RSVP notifications and guest inquiries.

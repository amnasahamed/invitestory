The Final 30 Days Before Your Wedding: Emergency Checklist & Last-Minute To-Dos

Published by InviteStory Editorial · 5 min read · Updated August 2026

The final month before your wedding day is all about finalizing logistics, double-checking vendor bookings, and keeping stress levels low. Here is your essential 30-day countdown checklist.

Week 4 (30 to 22 Days Out)

  • Lock Final RSVP Headcount: Export your digital RSVP submissions from your InviteStory dashboard to share with caterers and decorators.
  • Final Outfit Fitting: Try on your full wedding outfit with your shoes, jewelry, and undergarments to check comfort and movement.
  • Confirm Music Playlists: Share entrance tracks and dance numbers with your DJ and Sangeet team.

Week 3 (21 to 15 Days Out)

  • Confirm Vendor Arrival Times: Call makeup artists, photographers, decorators, and venue managers to verify setup schedules.
  • Assemble Wedding Day Emergency Kit: Pack safety pins, stain wipes, touch-up makeup, extra phone chargers, and pain relievers.
  • Break In Your Wedding Shoes: Wear your bridal shoes around the house with socks to prevent blisters on the big day.

Week 2 & Final Week (14 to 1 Days Out)

  • Send Travel Updates to Guests: Push a final reminder notification or WhatsApp message with your e-invite link and venue map pin.
  • Pamper & Rest: Get plenty of sleep, hydrate, and enjoy quality time with your partner and family!

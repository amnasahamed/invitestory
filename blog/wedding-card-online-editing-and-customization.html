Wedding Card Online Editing: How to Customize Names, Photos & RSVP Links

Published by InviteStory Editorial · 7 min read · Updated August 2026

Performing wedding card online editing is one of the biggest advantages of modern digital invitations. Unlike printed paper cards where a single typo can cost thousands of rupees in reprinting fees, marriage card editing online allows you to modify text, event times, and location links effortlessly.

1. Key Information to Check During Online Editing

When editing wedding card details, ensure these critical elements are accurate:

  • Spellings of Couple & Family Names: Verify groom, bride, parents, and grandparents' names and honorifics.
  • Google Maps Location Links: Test venue GPS links on mobile devices to verify directions lead straight to the main gate.
  • RSVP Deadline Dates: Set a clear RSVP deadline (typically 2 to 3 weeks before the wedding day) to lock in guest headcounts.
  • Background Music Track: Choose audio that complements your aesthetic, whether romantic instrumental acoustic or traditional Shehnai/Nadaswaram.

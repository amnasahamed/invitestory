Digital Wedding Card Etiquette: WhatsApp Sharing & RSVP Tracking

Published by InviteStory Editorial · 6 min read · Updated August 2026

Sending a digital wedding invitation link via WhatsApp, email, or Instagram is fast and convenient, but doing it with warmth, respect, and proper etiquette ensures your guests feel truly valued and excited to attend.

1. Perfect WhatsApp Message Template

When sharing your private invitestory.in/your-name link, pair it with a warm personal note rather than just dropping a bare URL:

"Dear [Family Name / Friend],

Together with our families, we request the honor of your presence as we celebrate our wedding day! ✨

Please tap our personal wedding link below to view the interactive invitation, event schedule, venue location on Google Maps, and kindly submit your RSVP:

👉 https://invitestory.in/demo-wedding

With warm regards,
[Bride] & [Groom]"

2. When to Send Your Digital Invitation Link

Send your main wedding website link 4 to 8 weeks before the wedding day. For out-of-town guests or destination weddings, send a Save-The-Date notification 3 to 6 months in advance.

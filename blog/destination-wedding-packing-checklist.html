Destination Wedding Packing Checklist: Everything You Need for a Multi-Day Event

Published by InviteStory Editorial · 6 min read · Updated August 2026

Comprehensive luggage packing list for brides and grooms traveling for destination weddings.

1. Outfit Luggage & Garment Bag Packing

Pack heavy outfits in sturdy garment bags, carry jewelry and wedding bands in hand luggage, and bring a portable steamer.

2. Important Documents & Backup Hardware

Keep marriage licenses, IDs, hotel vouchers, physical copies of contracts, and extra phone chargers in a designated travel pouch.

Digital Shadi Card Guide: WhatsApp Sharing & English Templates

Published by InviteStory Editorial · 7 min read · Updated August 2026

Creating a digital shadi card has become the preferred choice for modern Indian weddings. A whatsapp shadi card link delivers instant elegance, interactive Google Maps directions, ambient music, and digital RSVP management directly to your guests' phones.

1. Marriage Invitation Card Text in English for WhatsApp

When sharing your shadi card for whatsapp broadcasts, use this structured English message template:

"With the divine grace of the Almighty and blessings of our family,

We cordially invite you to celebrate the Shadi & Reception of our beloved son/daughter [Groom Name] & [Bride Name]. ✨

Please tap our digital shadi card link to view the complete event itinerary, venue location pin on Google Maps, and kindly submit your RSVP:

👉 https://invitestory.in/demo-shadi

We look forward to celebrating with you!

Warm Regards,
[Family Name / Parents]"

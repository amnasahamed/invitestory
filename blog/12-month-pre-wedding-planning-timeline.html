The Stress-Free 12-Month Pre-Wedding Checklist for Modern Couples

Published by InviteStory Editorial · 7 min read · Updated August 2026

Planning a wedding can feel overwhelming without a structured roadmap. Breaking your to-do list into manageable monthly goals helps you enjoy your engagement period while staying on schedule.

12 to 10 Months Before: Foundation & Budget

Set total budget, draft initial guest count, research venues, book wedding planner, and reserve primary ceremony locations.

9 to 8 Months Before: Key Vendors & Style

Book lead photographer, videographer, decorator, and caterers. Begin bridal wear research and select color themes.

7 to 6 Months Before: Digital Save-the-Dates

Schedule pre-wedding photoshoot. Send out digital Save-the-Date e-cards to out-of-town guests and lock in hotel block reservations.

5 to 4 Months Before: Outfits & Invitations

Finalize bridal and groom attire. Select your interactive digital wedding card template on InviteStory with live RSVP forms and Google Maps integration.

3 to 2 Months Before: Launch Invites & RSVPs

Distribute digital wedding card link via WhatsApp broadcasts and email. Track guest responses and dietary preferences in real time.

1 Month Before: Final Adjustments

Conduct final outfit fittings, review floor plans, lock catering counts based on digital RSVP data, and relax!

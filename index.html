About InviteStory

InviteStory helps couples create premium digital wedding invitation websites with Google Maps navigation, photo galleries, countdown timers, 1-click Google Calendar sync, and WhatsApp sharing. Built specifically for Indian weddings, couples select from 21 culturally-curated templates (Royal Palace, South Indian, Nikah, Walima, Hindu Muhurtham, Ghibli Animation, Beach, Garden), submit their wedding event details over WhatsApp, and receive a live custom web invitation within 24 hours.

Core Platform Features

  • Google Maps Navigation: One-tap interactive GPS directions to your wedding venue.
  • 1-Click Google Calendar Sync: Add the wedding date directly to guest mobile calendars.
  • Photo Galleries & Couple Stories: High-resolution WebP compressed photo carousels and animated couple journey timelines.
  • Countdown Timers: Dynamic live event countdown clock building anticipation for your big day.
  • WhatsApp Sharing & OG Cards: Rich visual preview cards when sharing your invitation link in WhatsApp chats.
  • Zero RSVP Friction: Designed as an announcement and digital hospitality card without awkward response buttons for elders.
  • Floating Background Music: User-controlled audio player featuring traditional or instrumental tracks.

Specialized Invitation Categories

  • Digital Wedding Invitations: Web invitations for modern Indian couples looking for single-link sharing on WhatsApp.
  • Muslim Nikah & Walima Invitations: Designs featuring emerald parchment, Arabic calligraphy, floral roses, and moonlit mandalas (Emerald Nikah, Noor-e-Zahra, Royal Reception).
  • Hindu Wedding Websites: Royal Rajasthani palace themes, mandapam vector art, and olive-gold paper envelope styles (Rajwada Royale, Marigold Bhavan, Rajmahal Palace).
  • South Indian Wedding Invites: Banana leaf accents, brass lamps, and traditional Telugu/Tamil typography (Toran Telugu, Kalyana Mandapam, Tamil Thirumana, Kerala Sands).
  • Christian & Kerala Backwater Invites: Ivory and gold coastal temple/church vibes (Kerala Sands).
  • Save-The-Date Digital Cards: Lock in guest calendars 3 to 6 months in advance with interactive countdown web announcements.

Refund & Editing Policy

Clear rules on refunds, order requirements, editing windows, and hosted link delivery for InviteStory services.

1 Strict No-Refund Policy

All sales and customization orders with InviteStory are final. Because every digital wedding invitation card is hand-crafted, personalized, and customized specifically with your names, dates, photos, venue details, and individual design choices, no refunds or order cancellations are offered once payment is processed and customization work has commenced.

Please ensure you review template live previews and confirm your chosen style before completing payment.

2 Order Requirements & WhatsApp Communication

To maintain clarity and accuracy, your customization requirements must be clearly documented via WhatsApp. You may send your details before or after payment; customization begins once payment and all required details have been received.

  • Text Messages Only: Names, dates, timings, venue addresses, Google Maps links, family names, audio track selections, and special text wording must be sent as clear text messages.
  • Non-Qualifying Requirements: Vague instructions, "beat-around-the-bush" statements, unwritten assumptions, phone calls, or audio voice notes will not be qualified or recognized as binding customization requirements.
  • Special Features: Special features beyond your selected package are available on request and cost extra based on your requirements. Contact us on WhatsApp for a quote before booking. We confirm the additional price, scope and any change to delivery timing before starting the custom work.
  • Final Confirmation: We build your first draft from the complete requirements confirmed in your WhatsApp chat. Please send names, dates, venues, photos and any special requests clearly before work begins.

3 24-Hour Revision & Editing Window

We want your custom invitation to be accurate and perfect for your big day.

Free revision requests for 24 hours after your first draft is delivered. Detail edits and text corrections are included during this window.
  • Covered Edits: Correction of spelling typos, adjusting event timings, updating Google Maps venue links, or swapping photos with valid high-resolution files.
  • Post-24h Requests: Any change or revision requested after the 24-hour revision window has passed will be subject to a nominal re-editing fee.
  • Scope of Edits: Edits apply to content adjustments within your selected template style. Requesting a complete change to a different template style after draft delivery will be treated as a new separate order.

4 Link Delivery, Managed Hosting & No Code Access

InviteStory delivers your digital wedding invitation as a live, interactive web link hosted on our high-speed Cloudflare global edge infrastructure (e.g. *.invitestory.in).

  • Hosted Service: You will receive a shareable invitation link to send to your guests on WhatsApp, Instagram, or email.
  • No Code Delivery: InviteStory provides a hosted digital service. Raw source code files (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, repository access, or source template files) strictly will not be delivered or transferred to clients under any circumstances.

5 Link Hosting Duration & Validity

Your hosted invitation link remains live, active, and accessible worldwide until 1 month after your event end date (event end date + 1 month validity). This ensures full access for all your pre-wedding and wedding festivities, plus ample time for post-wedding photo memories and guest revisits. Link extensions beyond this period can be renewed upon request for a nominal hosting fee.

6 Client Content & Photo Responsibility

The client is solely responsible for ensuring they possess full legal rights and permissions for all photos, audio tracks, and family text provided to InviteStory for custom inclusion. InviteStory is not liable for copyright issues arising from client-supplied media assets.

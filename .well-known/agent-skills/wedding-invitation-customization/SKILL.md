# Wedding Invitation Customization Skill

Use this skill to guide couples and event planners through customizing interactive wedding invitations on InviteStory.

## Available Themes & Styles

1. **Royal Rajasthani & Palace**: Diya Haveli, Rajmahal Palace, Gilded Hall, Rajwada Royale. Features royal arch reveals, wax seals, and traditional Shehnai background music.
2. **Islamic Nikah & Walima**: Emerald Nikah, Noor-e-Zahra, Meadow Nikah. Elegant Urdu/Arabic calligraphy motifs with pastel floristry.
3. **South Indian & Telugu**: Kalyana Mandapam, Toran Telugu, Shubha Vivaham, Ganesha Gopuram. Incorporates auspicious temple bells, mango leaf torans, and Google Maps venue directions.
4. **Botanical & Minimalist**: Ever After Bloom, Lotus Leaf Bengaluru, Sage Parchment, Ivory Waltz.
5. **Modern & Quirky**: Slide to Shaadi, Grand Line Voyage, Ghibli Portrait & Anime selfie themes.

## Customization Workflow

1. Select template ID from catalog.
2. Prepare event itinerary: Date, Time, Venue with Google Maps link, Groom & Bride family names.
3. Submit customization details via WhatsApp (+91 82815 83882) or web form.
4. Review interactive web draft delivered within 24 hours (12 hours with Express).

Official terms governing digital invitation creation, payment terms, and electronic delivery timelines.

1 Overview & Service Agreement

InviteStory provides premium, interactive digital wedding invitation templates delivered as hosted web links. By making a payment on our website or through Razorpay, you agree to these Terms & Conditions.

2 Digital Service Delivery Policy

Electronic Delivery: All InviteStory products are digital goods. No physical paper cards are shipped to physical mailing addresses.
  • Standard Delivery Timeline: Your first draft is delivered electronically via WhatsApp or Email within 48 hours after payment and receipt of all required wedding details and photos.
  • Express Delivery: With the Express add-on or Dearly, your first draft is delivered within 24 hours after payment and complete details. Review and approve your draft before sharing.
  • Delivery Channel: Delivery is completed by sending your shareable *.invitestory.in web link directly to your registered WhatsApp number or Email address.

Free revision requests for 24 hours after your first draft is delivered. Hosting extensions beyond this duration are available upon request for a nominal fee.

Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 9 November 2026

InviteStory respects your right to control your personal data. This page explains what data we hold when you contact us on Instagram, Facebook or our website, and how you can ask us to delete it.

What data we may hold

When you message or comment on our Instagram (@invitestory.in) or Facebook Page (Invitestory.in):

  • Your Instagram or Facebook username and the account ID Meta provides to us
  • The messages, comments and any photos, reels or links you send us
  • Details you choose to share, such as your name, wedding date, events and WhatsApp number

We use this only to reply to you, answer questions about our invitations, and follow up on your order. Our automated assistant uses an AI service (OpenAI) to write replies. Your messages are processed only to generate those replies and are not used to advertise to you.

When you order through our website or WhatsApp:

  • Name, email address and WhatsApp/phone number
  • Event details you provide for your invitation (names, dates, venues, photos)

We never sell, rent or trade your personal data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How to request deletion

Send us a deletion request using any of the options below. Please include the words "Delete my data" and the Instagram username, Facebook name or phone number you used to contact us.

WhatsApp: +91 82815 83882

DM @invitestory.in on Instagram

What happens next

  1. We confirm your request within 3 working days. We may ask a simple question to verify that the request comes from you.
  2. Within 30 days we delete your messages, comments, contact details and conversation history from our own systems: our customer records, our automation server and its logs.
  3. We send you a confirmation once the deletion is complete.

Things to know

  • Messages inside Instagram and Facebook: copies of your conversation also exist in Meta's apps. You can delete them on your side, and we delete our side of the conversation where Meta allows it. Meta's own data is governed by Meta's privacy policy.
  • Active or completed orders: if you have placed an order, we may keep invoice and payment records for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires. Everything else is deleted.
  • Published invitations: if we created an invitation for you, deleting your data also takes your invitation link offline.
  • Removing app access: you can also remove any app's access to your Facebook account at any time under Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about The Dearly Collection.

How does first-draft delivery work?

Once you confirm your booking, our concierge designer connects with you on WhatsApp to collect your couple details, wedding timeline, photos, and music preference. Your first-draft timing and any delivery fee are confirmed before payment. Timing starts after payment and complete details. Existing orders keep their purchased promise. Review and approve it before sharing.

How does Email RSVP work?

Your invitation includes a beautifully designed RSVP module. When guests fill out their names, attendance status, guest count, and notes, the system sends an email notification to your designated inbox with guest names and an option to download an Excel file. There is no separate dashboard.

Is the Photo Gallery optional?

Yes. Your photo gallery is included in the Dearly price when requested. You can include your photos and story moments, or leave the gallery out for a simpler invitation.

How do guests open the invitation?

When guests open your link on WhatsApp, Instagram, or iMessage, they are greeted by an elegant wax-sealed envelope. A gentle tap breaks the seal with a smooth animation and unfolds your wedding story.

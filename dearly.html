Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about The Dearly Collection.

How does the 24-hour delivery process work?

Once you confirm your booking, our concierge designer connects with you on WhatsApp to collect your couple details, wedding timeline, photos, and music preference. Within 24 hours of receiving your assets, your live, private link is ready to share.

How does Email RSVP work?

Your invitation includes a beautifully designed RSVP module. When guests fill out their names, attendance status, guest count, and notes, the system sends an email notification directly to your designated inbox so you can effortlessly track headcounts.

Is the Photo Gallery optional?

Yes! You can choose to include your engagement photos and story moments, or keep the invitation focused strictly on the event schedule and venue details if you prefer a minimalist look.

How do guests open the invitation?

When guests open your link on WhatsApp, Instagram, or iMessage, they are greeted by an elegant wax-sealed envelope. A gentle tap breaks the seal with a smooth animation and unfolds your wedding story.

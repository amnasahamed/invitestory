Data Protection & Trust

Privacy Policy

How InviteStory collects, uses, and safeguards your personal details, photos, and event information.

1 Information We Collect

To craft and deliver your personalized digital wedding card, we collect the following details provided voluntarily by you via our website or WhatsApp:

  • Personal Details: Full Name, Email Address, and WhatsApp / Mobile Phone Number.
  • Event Information: Bride & Groom names, Wedding event dates, venue addresses, and Google Maps location links.
  • Media Assets: Couple photos, background audio tracks, and custom message text.
  • Payment Information: Payment processing is securely handled directly through Razorpay (supporting domestic UPI, Netbanking, and international credit/debit cards in USD & INR). InviteStory does not store your card details, UPI PINs, or bank credentials.

2 How We Use Your Information

The wedding details and media you provide are used to fulfil your digital invitation order:

  • Customizing your interactive wedding invitation website link.
  • Sending you draft previews, updates, and order confirmations via WhatsApp or Email.
  • Providing hosting for your invitation link until 1 month after your event end date on our high-performance, secure cloud infrastructure.

3 Data Privacy & Non-Sharing Commitment

We Value Your Privacy: InviteStory will NEVER sell, rent, or trade your personal information, photos, or contact details to any third-party marketing companies or advertisers.

Your invitation is shared through a web link. Anyone with the link can open it, including people it is forwarded to.

4 Data Security & Hosting

All data and hosted digital wedding cards are transmitted securely over SSL (HTTPS) and hosted on high-performance, secure cloud servers. We implement industry-standard administrative and technical security measures to prevent unauthorized access.

5 Website Analytics & Browser Storage

Our website uses Google Analytics, Meta Pixel and Microsoft Clarity to measure visits, interactions and conversions, understand how the site is used, and improve our advertising and customer experience. These providers may process browser, device and interaction information through their services.

We use browser storage to remember your currency, saved designs and browsing choices. Names entered for a preview are stored in your browser tab. If you choose WhatsApp saving and give permission, we also store your number, preview names, selected design and consent in our staff portal to restore your preview and provide the agreed reminders. Confirm your number through WhatsApp before automated delivery. Reply STOP to stop reminders. Campaign parameters and advertising click identifiers may be retained to attribute enquiries and purchases to their source and included with order information.

You can clear stored site data through your browser settings. For questions about your information, contact us on WhatsApp.

Privacy Policy

How InviteStory collects, uses, and safeguards your personal details, photos, and event information.

1 Information We Collect

To craft and deliver your personalized digital wedding card, we collect the following details provided voluntarily by you via our website or WhatsApp:

  • Personal Details: Full Name, Email Address, and WhatsApp / Mobile Phone Number.
  • Event Information: Bride & Groom names, Wedding event dates, venue addresses, and Google Maps location links.
  • Media Assets: Couple photos, background audio tracks, and custom message text.
  • Payment Information: Payment processing is securely handled directly through Razorpay. InviteStory does not store your card details, UPI PINs, or bank credentials.

2 How We Use Your Information

Your details are strictly used to fulfill your digital invitation order:

  • Customizing your interactive wedding invitation website link.
  • Sending you draft previews, updates, and order confirmations via WhatsApp or Email.
  • Providing 365-day hosting for your invitation link on our secure Vercel infrastructure.

3 Data Privacy & Non-Sharing Commitment

🔒 We Value Your Privacy: InviteStory will NEVER sell, rent, or trade your personal information, photos, or contact details to any third-party marketing companies or advertisers.

Your private wedding link is only accessible to guests with whom you share your unique URL.

4 Data Security & Hosting

All data and hosted digital wedding cards are transmitted securely over SSL (HTTPS) and hosted on high-performance Vercel cloud servers. We implement industry-standard administrative and technical security measures to prevent unauthorized access.

InviteStory Instagram App Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026
Last updated: 8 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how InviteStory collects, uses, stores and shares information processed through its Instagram messaging application.

1 Purpose of the App

InviteStory uses Meta’s Instagram API to respond to messages and enquiries sent to the official InviteStory Instagram account.

The application may:

  • Acknowledge incoming Instagram messages.
  • Identify the customer’s enquiry or intent.
  • Provide information about InviteStory designs and services.
  • Collect a WhatsApp number voluntarily provided by the customer.
  • Direct customers to InviteStory’s website or WhatsApp team.
  • Notify InviteStory staff when human assistance is required.
  • Record enquiry and conversion information for customer support and sales analysis.

Customers may be informed when they are interacting with an automated assistant.

2 Information We Collect

Depending on the customer’s interaction, the application may process:

  • Instagram account ID, profile name and username provided by Meta.
  • Messages sent to InviteStory, including text, reactions, replies, shared posts or reels.
  • Message timestamps and conversation identifiers.
  • Name and WhatsApp or mobile number voluntarily provided by the customer.
  • Wedding date, design preference, language, budget or other enquiry information.
  • Whether an enquiry was assigned to an InviteStory staff member.
  • Enquiry status, order status and purchase or conversion status.
  • Technical information required to operate, secure and troubleshoot the application.

InviteStory does not request Instagram passwords, payment PINs or banking credentials through Instagram messages.

3 How We Use Information

We use this information to:

  • Respond to Instagram enquiries.
  • Maintain the context of a customer conversation.
  • Detect duplicate enquiries.
  • Provide relevant designs, prices and ordering assistance.
  • Connect customers with InviteStory’s WhatsApp or human support team.
  • Send an approved WhatsApp message when a customer has provided their WhatsApp number.
  • Track whether an enquiry was handled or converted into an order.
  • Monitor application reliability and prevent abuse.
  • Improve InviteStory’s customer service and automated responses.

Automated systems or AI services may be used to classify messages, identify customer intent and generate suggested responses. Important order or support matters may be transferred to an InviteStory staff member.

4 Services Used to Process Information

InviteStory may use the following service providers:

  • Meta Platforms: To receive and respond to Instagram messages.
  • n8n: InviteStory’s self-hosted workflow automation system.
  • Gupshup: To deliver approved WhatsApp Business messages.
  • InviteStory’s CRM or ERP: To manage enquiries, conversations, orders and staff assignments.
  • AI service providers configured by InviteStory: To classify enquiries or generate response suggestions.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers: To securely run the application and store operational information.

These providers process information only as necessary to deliver their respective services. Information may be processed in countries other than the customer’s country of residence.

InviteStory does not sell or rent Instagram conversation data, mobile numbers or customer information.

5 WhatsApp Handoff

Customers may choose to continue an enquiry on WhatsApp by:

  • Selecting the WhatsApp link displayed in Instagram; or
  • Voluntarily providing their WhatsApp number in the Instagram conversation.

When a customer provides a number, InviteStory may use it to send an approved WhatsApp message relating to that enquiry. Customers can ask InviteStory to stop follow-up communication at any time.

6 Data Retention

InviteStory retains enquiry and conversation information only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide customer support.
  • Manage an enquiry or order.
  • Maintain business and transaction records.
  • Prevent fraud or misuse.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.
  • Resolve complaints or disputes.

Information that is no longer required may be deleted or anonymised.

7 Data Security

InviteStory uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information, including:

  • HTTPS encryption during transmission.
  • Restricted access to authorised staff and services.
  • Access-controlled server infrastructure.
  • Secure storage of application credentials.
  • Monitoring and backups required for service reliability.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8 Customer Rights and Data Deletion

Customers may request access to, correction of or deletion of information connected to their Instagram enquiry.

To submit a request:

  1. Contact InviteStory on WhatsApp at +91 82815 83882.
  2. Send the words “Instagram data request.”
  3. Provide the Instagram username associated with the request.
  4. Specify whether the request concerns access, correction or deletion.

InviteStory may request reasonable verification before processing the request to prevent unauthorised access or deletion.

Information will be deleted unless it must be retained for an active order, transaction record, fraud prevention, dispute or legal obligation.

Customers can also manage permissions granted to applications through their Instagram or Meta account settings.

9 Children’s Information

The application is intended for people making wedding invitation and related service enquiries. InviteStory does not knowingly collect personal information from children through this application.

10 Changes to This Policy

InviteStory may update this Privacy Policy when its application, service providers or legal obligations change. The latest version will remain available at this URL with its updated effective date.
